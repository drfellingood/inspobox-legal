灵感盒子隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 27 日

灵感盒子是一款在你手机上记录灵感的工具。我们的原则很简单：你记下的东西只属于你，只存在你的设备上。

我们不收集任何数据

灵感盒子不需要注册账号，没有服务器，不包含广告、统计分析或任何第三方追踪工具。你记录的文字、照片、录音和链接都只保存在你 iPhone 的本地存储中，我们无法看到，也不会上传到任何地方。

权限的用途

  • 麦克风：只在你点「哼一段」并开始录音时使用，录下的音频保存在本机。
  • 相机：只在你选择拍照或「对着它拍」时使用，照片保存在本机。
  • 相册：只在你选择从相册添加照片时，读取你选中的那张照片。
  • 通知：只在你打开「每天提醒我翻一翻」时使用。提醒由手机在本地定时发出，不经过任何服务器。

你可以随时在 iPhone 的「设置」中关闭这些权限，关闭后其他功能不受影响。

分享与导出

只有在你主动点击「分享」或「导出全部灵感」时，内容才会通过系统分享面板发送到你选择的位置。发送之后，内容将受到你选择的应用或服务的隐私政策约束。

打开链接

当你点击灵感中的链接时，App 会用系统浏览器打开该网页。网页由第三方提供，适用其自己的隐私政策。

数据的保存与删除

你可以在 App 内随时删除任何一条灵感。删除 App 会同时删除其中保存的所有内容。若你开启了 iPhone 的 iCloud 云备份，系统备份中可能包含本 App 的数据，这由 Apple 依照其隐私政策管理。

儿童

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集任何人的个人信息。

政策变更

如果本政策发生变化，我们会更新本页面和生效日期。

Inspo Box Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026

Inspo Box is a tool for capturing ideas on your phone. Our principle is simple: what you save belongs to you and stays on your device.

We don’t collect any data

Inspo Box has no account sign-up, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking. The text, photos, audio recordings and links you save are stored only in your iPhone’s local storage. We can’t see them and they are never uploaded.

How permissions are used

  • Microphone: used only when you tap “Hum it” and start recording. Recordings stay on your device.
  • Camera: used only when you take a photo or use “Shoot it”. Photos stay on your device.
  • Photos: used only to read the photo you choose to add.
  • Notifications: used only if you turn on the daily Revisit reminder. Your phone schedules it locally; no server is involved.

You can turn these off at any time in iPhone Settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

Sharing and export

Content leaves the app only when you tap Share or Export all notes, through the system share sheet, to a destination you choose. After that, the privacy policy of that app or service applies.

Opening links

Links you saved open in the system browser. Those websites are run by third parties and have their own privacy policies.

Keeping and deleting data

You can delete any note in the app at any time. Deleting the app deletes everything saved in it. If you use iCloud Backup, your device backup may include this app’s data, which Apple manages under its own privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
