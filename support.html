灵感盒子支持

灵感盒子不需要账号，所有功能都可以离线使用。

常见问题

灵感被分错了盒子

点卡片左上角的彩色标签，就能换到别的盒子。你手动选过的盒子，之后修改文字也不会被改掉。发送前也可以在输入框上方直接选好盒子。

录不了音 / 拍不了照

请到 iPhone「设置」→「灵感盒子」，打开麦克风或相机权限。

录音播放声音很小

请确认手机没有连接其他蓝牙设备，并调高媒体音量。灵感盒子在静音模式下也能播放录音。

换手机怎么办

所有内容只存在你的手机上。换手机前，请在「设置」→「导出全部灵感」导出一份备份；如果开启了 iCloud 云备份，用备份恢复新手机也会带上数据。

「翻一翻」里为什么没有内容

只有记下两天以上、还没实现的灵感，才会被翻出来。每天最多翻出 5 条。

Inspo Box Support

Inspo Box needs no account and works fully offline.

Common questions

A note landed in the wrong box

Tap the coloured tab at the top-left of the card to move it. A box you pick yourself is never changed when you edit the text later. You can also pick a box above the input before sending.

Recording or the camera doesn’t work

Go to iPhone Settings → Inspo Box and turn on Microphone or Camera.

Playback is quiet

Check that no other Bluetooth device is connected and turn up media volume. Recordings play even in silent mode.

Switching phones

Everything is stored only on your phone. Before switching, use Settings → Export all notes to save a backup. Restoring a new phone from iCloud Backup also brings your notes across.

Revisit is empty

Only notes older than two days that you haven’t acted on come back, up to five a day.
